## Fonts and vendor resources

The stylesheet requests DM Sans and IBM Plex Mono through Google Fonts, with system fallbacks. Those externally served fonts have their own licensing and provider terms; no font files are included here.

Vendor executables, firmware bundles and the HFD manual PDF are omitted from this public distribution. Help falls back to official vendor links. A locally supplied optional PDF is not a redistribution grant and should not be committed without permission.

Protocol descriptions and synthetic fixtures document behavior and formats. Do not add private measurements, user projects, device serials, support credentials or third-party binaries as test data.
